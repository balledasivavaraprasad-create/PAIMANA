# Copy and UX Writing Guidelines

## Tone

Professional, direct, evidence-aware.

Avoid marketing clichés.

## Preferred vocabulary

Use:

- monitored
- observed
- detected
- predicted
- comparable projects
- peer cohort
- possible explanation
- evidence
- uncertainty
- recommendation
- approved
- observed outcome

Avoid:

- magical
- revolutionary
- guaranteed
- autonomous government decision-maker
- 100% accurate
- explains the cause (unless actually verified)

## Status wording

### Data

`Fresh`, `Delayed`, `Stale`, `Unavailable`

### Risk

`Low`, `Moderate`, `High`, `Critical`

### Investigation

`Queued`, `Investigating`, `Ready for review`, `Pending approval`, `Concluded`, `Insufficient evidence`

### Recommendation

`Candidate`, `Validated`, `Pending approval`, `Approved`, `Rejected`

### Automation

`Queued`, `Sent`, `Retrying`, `Failed`, `Dead letter`

## Numbers

Always show units.

Examples:

- ₹123 Cr
- 72.4 DPHIS
- 5.6 months
- 64% expenditure

Use tabular numerals for dense numeric layouts.

## Causal wording

Preferred:

> “Evidence is consistent with…”

> “The current evidence suggests…”

> “Comparable projects show…”

Avoid:

> “The contractor caused…”

unless directly supported and verified by domain evidence.
